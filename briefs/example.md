# 브리프

- Subject: How FAA Opspec A134 is changing pilot hiring forever
- Audience: Student pilots, Certified flight instructors, Part 135 pilots, and aviators aiming for Part 121 major airlines
- Target duration: 9 minutes
- Solution: A strategic roadmap showing pilots how to align their training, logbooks, and interview prep with new AAM/eVTOL pathways, SMS frameworks, and automation-focused hiring standards.
- Essential: 
OpSpec A134 Overview: Brief context on the February 2026 FAA update.

Powered-Lift & AAM Demand: How eVTOL commercial operations are opening new cadet-to-cockpit pathways.

Evolution of Flight Hours: The hiring shift from raw "stick-and-rudder" time to SVO (Simplified Vehicle Operations) and automation management.

Remote Pilot (RPIC) Careers: Expansion of Part 135 BVLOS ground control operator roles.

Part 121 Airline Hiring Changes: Powered-Lift logbook credits, SMS/TEM scenario-based interviews, and CRM-focused simulator evaluations.
- Not include: 
Dry recitations of FAA regulatory code or dense legal jargon.

Outdated, pre-2026 hiring assumptions (e.g., focusing solely on the 1,500-hour rule).

Generic "how to get your Private Pilot License" intro steps.

Unverified rumors regarding specific airline hiring quotas.
- Final action: Prompt viewers to comment on whether they plan to pursue a Powered-Lift rating
